What Orvia does

Ad block

Block ads and pop-ups while you browse, using filters stored on your device.

Incognito tabs

Browse without adding pages to local history. Shield is a separate privacy layer.

Anonymity Shield

Limit screenshots and leftover session data such as cookies and WebView storage.

Secure DNS

Encrypted DNS lookups via Cloudflare. This is not a full VPN: page traffic is not tunneled and your IP stays visible.
