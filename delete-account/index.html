Data deletion

Orvia: Private Browser

Effective date: September 14, 2026 · Developer: VIET - NHAT HUMAN RESOURCES SUPPLY

Orvia does not create an online account. Bookmarks, history, tabs, downloads list, cookies, and settings live on your device. Use the steps below to delete them.

Delete browsing data inside the App

  1. Open Orvia.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Clear browsing data and confirm.

This wipes tabs, bookmarks, history, the downloads list, cookies, and cache on the device. It cannot be undone.

Delete everything the App stored

Option A — Clear storage (keep the App installed):

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Go to Apps → Orvia.
  3. Tap Storage & cache.
  4. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data) and confirm.

The App will start as if newly installed.

Option B — Uninstall:

  1. Long-press the Orvia icon, or open Settings → Apps → Orvia.
  2. Choose Uninstall and confirm.

Uninstalling removes the App and its locally stored data. Files you already saved to Downloads may remain in device storage.

Ads, analytics, and Play purchases

Clearing Orvia does not by itself erase advertising, analytics, or purchase records held by Google or mediation partners. Use Google account / Ads settings and Play’s refund or purchase tools. For DNS lookups processed while Secure DNS was on, see Cloudflare’s policy.
