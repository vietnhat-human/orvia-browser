Common questions

Is Secure DNS a VPN?
No. It only encrypts DNS lookups (Cloudflare 1.1.1.1). Your IP stays visible and page traffic is not tunneled. See Terms and Privacy Policy.

How do I clear history, cookies, or bookmarks?
In Orvia, use Settings → Clear browsing data, or see the Data deletion page.

Why do I see ads?
Orvia is free and supported by ads. If an ad-free purchase is offered, it is billed through Google Play.

A website asked for camera, mic, or location.
That request comes from the site, not from Orvia’s own features. You can tap Deny.

How do I send feedback from the App?
Open Settings → Feedback. You can also write to the email above.
